W2-PM3: MALTEGO-BASED FOOTPRINTING

1. Overview

This exercise involved using Maltego to perform footprinting and Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) investigation on Networkwalks.

The objective was to understand how Maltego can collect, visualise, and map relationships between publicly available digital information, including domains, websites, email addresses, and people.

2. Practical Implementation

I launched Maltego and created a new graph to begin the footprinting exercise.

The graph was used to explore relationships involving Networkwalks Academy and the networkwalks.com domain.

3. Observations and Results

The graph generated several entities and relationships, including:

* Organisation: New Networkwalks Academy appeared as an organisation entity.
* Person: Waqas Karim CCIE appeared as a person entity connected to the academy through an employee relationship.
* Website and domain information: Multiple website and domain entities were displayed in the graph.
* Email information: The email address info@networkwalks.com appeared as an entity connected to the domain.
* Relationship mapping: Maltego displayed connections between the discovered entities, making it easier to visualise potential relationships.

These observations represent the output displayed in the Maltego graph and were not independently verified.

4. Learning Outcomes

This exercise provided practical experience in:

* Creating and working with Maltego graphs.
* Understanding entities and relationships.
* Using visual link analysis for footprinting.
* Exploring publicly available information.
* Understanding the role of OSINT in the reconnaissance phase of a security assessment.

5. Conclusion

The exercise demonstrated how Maltego can be used to gather and visualise publicly available information about a target’s digital presence. Its graphical representation of entities and relationships provides a useful approach to understanding connections that may not be immediately obvious through individual searches.
